Google Sheets Sync

To send scans directly to a Google Sheet:

  1. Create a Google Form with one "Short Answer" question.
  2. Go to Responses -> Link to Sheets.
  3. Click the 3 dots in the top right -> Get pre-filled link.
  4. Enter a dummy answer (e.g., "TEST") and click Get Link -> Copy Link.
  5. Paste that full URL below.
